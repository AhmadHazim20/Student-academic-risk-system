# AI-Assisted Student Academic Performance Risk Identification System

## 1. Description of Selected Case Study

This project explores the use of artificial intelligence to assist in identifying students who may be at risk of poor academic performance. The system is intended to support lecturers and academic advisors by helping them review relevant academic indicators and identify students who may require additional academic support.

The AI-assisted approach is intended to support decision-making rather than replace human judgement. Predictions should be reviewed by authorised staff before any follow-up action is taken. The project also considers potential risks, quality requirements, stakeholder responsibilities, and the limitations of using AI in an educational setting.
