# 9. AI-BASED SOLUTION RECOMMENDATION

## 9.1 Proposed Solution

The proposed solution is a web based with an AI assistance that can analyse selected student academic performance and provide justified recommendations to the authorised users.

The system Shall receive academic data input by the authorised users such as lecturers and academic advisors, validate the input whether it will contract with system validation, analyse the relevant information, generate a risk recommendation based on the information and then it will be display to the users along with the supporting evidence. Then the lecturers and academic advisors will review the recommendation and will provide a human decision that will be recorded by the system for future uses.

## 9.2 Ai Suitability

AI assistance in the web based system is suitable because academic performance may be calculate by a complex combinations of multiple indicators. A student may have not shown an extremely low mark but their behavior in the academic performance matter such as class attendance, missing assignment and slowly declining performance.

AI machine learning may potentially discover those indicators that are difficult to identify with human eyes.

### Limitations

The proposed solution has several limitations that are identified as:

#### 9.2.1 Dataset Limitations

- A small dataset may produce unreliable predictions that is unusable.

#### 9.2.2 Historical Bias

- Historical academic decisions may contain bias that would eventually be learn by the AI and produce a bias result.

#### 9.2.3 Explainability Limitations

- Some models may be difficult to explain clearly.

#### 9.2.4 Changing Student Behavior

- Change of pattern from student behavior may change over time and affect the predictions.

#### 9.2.5 Human dependency

- The system still require academic staff to interpret the recommendations.

#### 9.2.6 Privacy

- Student data require appropriate protection from unauthorised user.

## 9.3 Final Recommendation

The AI solution is recommended as an assistive screening system in which it only provides suggestions or recommendations, but the decision is purely made by humans. The explanation as below:

### 9.3.1 Human Oversight remains mandatory

The AI assistance may have provide the user with classification as low risk, medium risk or high risk, but a lecturer or academic advisor must review the recommended student by the evidence and the supporting factor before deciding what support the student needs.

### 9.3.2 Student data is protected

The system use the student personal academic data to provide a recommendation and prediction, these data must only be accessible only to authorised user such as lecturer and academic advisor.

### 9.3.3 Model performance is evaluated

The AI prediction need to me tested regularly to determine whether the data produced is reliable and accurate.

### 9.3.4 Non-AI baseline is also evaluated

The AI-assisted system should be compared with a simpler method that is non-AI such as attendance, examination marks and missing assignments.

### 9.3.5 Predictions are traceable

The system should provide the supporting factors as to why it classified the student as a certain risk category. This is for the user to review the evidence and made a decision on to accept the AI prediction or how to help the student.

### 9.3.6 Monitoring is implemented

The system should be checked regularly after deployment to identify problems, such as declining prediction accuracy, repeated errors or changes in student data.
